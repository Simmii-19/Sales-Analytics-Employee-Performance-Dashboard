# Stakeholder Analysis

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of stakeholder analysis is to identify the people who may use, influence, or be affected by the proposed Sales Analytics & Employee Performance Dashboard.

This analysis helps define stakeholder expectations, information needs, responsibilities, and communication requirements.

**Note:** The stakeholder roles below are simulated for this educational portfolio project. They do not represent actual interviews or confirmed company stakeholders.

## 2. Stakeholder Identification

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest in the Project |
|---|---|---|
| Sales Manager | Monitors sales performance | Revenue, gross profit, sales trends |
| Regional Manager | Monitors territory performance | Regional comparisons and territory trends |
| Product Manager | Monitors product performance | Product revenue, categories, profitability |
| Senior Management | Reviews overall performance | Executive KPIs and business summaries |
| Business Analyst | Understands and documents business needs | Requirements, scope, validation |
| Data Analyst | Prepares and analyses data | SQL queries, data quality, dashboards |

## 3. Stakeholder Requirements and Expectations

### Sales Manager
**Information Required:**
- Total revenue.
- Gross profit and profit margin.
- Monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Total orders and average order value.

**Expectation:** A clear view of overall sales performance to identify areas that may require investigation.

### Regional Manager
**Information Required:**
- Revenue by territory.
- Gross profit by territory.
- Regional performance comparisons.

**Expectation:** Ability to compare territories and investigate regional differences.

### Product Manager
**Information Required:**
- Revenue by product.
- Revenue by product category.
- Product-level gross profit.
- Top-performing products.

**Expectation:** Visibility into product and category performance.

### Senior Management
**Information Required:**
- Overall revenue.
- Gross profit.
- Profit margin.
- Sales performance trends.

**Expectation:** A concise executive overview of key business performance indicators.

### Business Analyst
**Responsibilities:**
- Identify stakeholder needs.
- Document business requirements.
- Define project scope.
- Validate requirements with stakeholders.
- Support acceptance criteria and business validation.

### Data Analyst
**Responsibilities:**
- Explore and prepare data.
- Write SQL queries.
- Validate analytical results.
- Develop Power BI dashboards.
- Present findings through reporting.

## 5. Communication Plan

| Stakeholder | Information to Communicate | Proposed Method |
|---|---|---|
| Sales Manager | Revenue and profitability reports | Dashboard review |
| Regional Manager | Territory comparisons | Regional dashboard review |
| Product Manager | Product performance reports | Product dashboard review |
| Senior Management | Executive KPI summary | Executive dashboard |
| Business Analyst | Requirements and scope | Requirements documentation |
| Data Analyst | Data definitions and validation | Technical documentation |

## 6. Key Stakeholder Expectations

The proposed solution should:
- Provide consistent KPI definitions.
- Present clear and understandable reports.
- Support relevant filtering and comparisons.
- Separate Internet Sales and Reseller Sales where their business metrics differ.
- Use validated data and calculations.
- Help users investigate their business questions.

## 7. Summary
Stakeholder analysis provides a foundation for gathering business requirements and designing the dashboard around user information needs.

The identified stakeholder expectations will be used as inputs for the Business Requirements Document (BRD) and Functional Requirements Document (FRD).
