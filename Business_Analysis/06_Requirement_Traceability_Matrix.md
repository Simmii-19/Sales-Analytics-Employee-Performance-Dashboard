## 1. Introduction

The Requirement Traceability Matrix (RTM) is used to track business requirements throughout the project lifecycle.

It connects business requirements, functional requirements, user stories, and test cases.

The purpose is to ensure that every identified business requirement is addressed by the proposed dashboard functionality and has a corresponding validation approach.

## 2. Objectives of RTM

- Track all identified business requirements.
- Connect business needs with system functionality.
- Ensure requirements are covered by test cases.
- Identify missing or incomplete requirements.
- Monitor implementation and validation status.
- Reduce the risk of requirements being overlooked.

## 6. Traceability Process

The proposed traceability process is:

1. Identify business requirements in the BRD.
2. Map business requirements to functional requirements in the FRD.
3. Link requirements to relevant user stories.
4. Define test scenarios and expected results.
5. Validate the dashboard against the requirements.
6. Update implementation and testing statuses.
7. Document any gaps or changes.

## 7. Project Benefits

The RTM helps:

- Maintain alignment between business needs and dashboard features.
- Ensure requirements are covered by testing.
- Identify missing functionality.
- Support change management.
- Improve communication between analysts, developers, and testers.
- Provide visibility into project progress.

## 8. Assumptions and Limitations

- Requirement IDs are based on the project's BRD and FRD.
- Test cases are proposed and have not been formally executed.
- Status values must be updated after checking the actual dashboard.
- Stakeholder approval has not been obtained.
- The matrix is designed for this portfolio case study.

## 9. Conclusion

The Requirement Traceability Matrix connects business requirements with functional requirements, user stories, and test cases.

It helps ensure that the proposed Sales Analytics & Employee Performance Dashboard addresses the identified business needs and provides a structured approach to implementation and validation.
